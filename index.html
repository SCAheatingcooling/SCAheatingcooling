Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common HVAC Questions

How fast can you get to my home?

We offer same-day appointments whenever possible during business hours, and 24/7 response for true heating or cooling emergencies. Call (573) 317-7239 and we'll give you a clear ETA.

Do you Provide free estimates?

Yes — estimates on new system installations and replacements are always free. For diagnostic visits on existing equipment, we'll quote a flat service-call rate up front before any work begins.

What brands of HVAC equipment do you service?

We service all major brands including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Goodman, Rheem, Bryant, York, American Standard, and more. If you're unsure, call us with your model number.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. SCA Heating & Cooling is fully licensed and insured to protect your home and our technicians on every job.

How often should I have my HVAC system serviced?

We recommend a tune-up twice a year — once in spring for your AC and once in fall for your heating system. Regular maintenance extends equipment life and prevents costly breakdowns. Ask us about our maintenance plans.

Do you offer financing on new systems?

Yes, we have financing options available for qualifying customers on full system replacements. Call or fill out the booking form and we'll walk you through the details.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, check, and all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Discover, American Express).

Do you offer warranties on your work?

Yes. New equipment is covered by manufacturer warranties, and our workmanship is backed by our satisfaction guarantee. We'll go over specifics in writing before any installation.
